# Library desk reservations

Library members can reserve a study desk for up to two hours each day. Reservations are free and must be made at least one hour before the start time.

## Book a desk

Sign in to the library website and open **Desk reservations**. Choose a date, a start time, and an available desk, then select **Confirm reservation**. The confirmation page shows your reservation number and the time your booking ends.

## Arrive at the library

Check in at the front desk within 15 minutes of your start time using your reservation number. After 15 minutes, an unchecked reservation is released so another member can use the desk.
